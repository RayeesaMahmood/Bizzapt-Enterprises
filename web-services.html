FAQs

Common questions,
honest answers.

Do you build mobile apps, or just web? +

Our Web Services focus on web applications and websites. That said, we build with React Native or cross-platform approaches on request — ask us and we'll give you an honest scope.

What if I already have a codebase? +

No problem. We regularly jump into existing codebases — auditing, refactoring, adding features, or migrating to a new stack. Share your repo and we'll give an honest assessment before quoting.

Can you work with our existing design team? +

Absolutely. If you have Figma files or a design system, we work from them. We're also happy to liaise directly with your designers to ensure pixel-perfect implementation.

How long does a typical project take? +

A landing site can take 1–2 weeks. A full-stack application typically ranges from 4–12 weeks depending on scope. We set clear timelines on the discovery call and stick to them.

Will I own the code when you're done? +

Yes — always. All code, assets, and repositories are 100% yours from the moment of delivery. No lock-in, no licence fees, no surprises.

Do you provide support after launch? +

Yes. All projects include a post-launch support window for bug fixes. Ongoing support retainers are available separately — ask us about our maintenance plans.
