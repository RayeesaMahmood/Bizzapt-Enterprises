Common Questions

Everything you wondered about.

Do I need to know design to work with you?

+

Not at all. We'll guide you through everything — from explaining what wireframes are to walking you through the final prototype. You just need to know what your business needs. We handle all the design thinking and execution.

How long does a typical UI/UX project take?

+

A 5-screen Starter project typically takes 5–7 working days. A full product design (15+ screens with research and testing) usually runs 3–5 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline estimate after our discovery call based on your specific scope.

Will I own the Figma files after the project?

+

Yes — 100%. Once payment is settled, all source files, assets, and prototypes are transferred to you. No ongoing licensing fees, no subscriptions, no restrictions. You can share them with any developer or future designer.

What if I don't like the initial designs?

+

That's what revision rounds are for. Starter includes 2 rounds, Growth includes unlimited revisions. We also do a thorough brief before starting to ensure we're designing in the right direction from day one — reducing surprises significantly.

Can you work with an existing brand or style guide?

+

Absolutely. If you have an existing brand identity, logo, or style guide we'll design strictly within those guidelines. If you only have a rough idea, we can help define the visual direction as part of the project.

Do you also build the website / app after designing it?

+

Yes! Bizzapt also offers Web Development and Website Creation services. Many clients hire us for the full end-to-end — design to development — which gives you the cleanest handoff possible since the same team understands both.
